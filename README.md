## Play here
[LittleJS Game Jam entry](https://eoinmcg.itch.io/bugandbee)

----
Something weird is happening in the microcosmos...

Angry insects are attacking everything...

Sounds like a job for Bug & Bee!

Controls:
Player 1: Arrows and [space] to shoot
Player 2: WASD and [f] to shoot
or use a gamepad
hold fire for a mega shot
[M] mutes music & sfx
[P] pause
